Security & Data Protection

Your Data Is Safe With Us

We understand the responsibility of handling your customer data. Our security practices are built into every aspect of our operation.

🔐 Access Controls

Role-based access and minimum-privilege permissions for all agents. We work within whatever access framework you define.

📄 Confidentiality

All agents sign individual NDAs. Mutual NDAs executed with every client before onboarding begins.

🛡️ Data Handling

No customer data stored on personal devices. All work performed through secured, access-controlled platforms.

🔍 Audit Trail

All customer interactions logged with timestamps for compliance review and quality assurance.

⚠️ Incident Response

Documented breach notification procedures. Any suspected data incident escalated and reported within 24 hours.

✅ Compliance

HIPAA-aware workflows, PHIPA/PIPEDA compliance capabilities, and BAA execution available for healthcare clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

What scheduling systems do you work with?+
We work with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve Dental, Carestream Dental, and most other major practice management systems. If yours isn't listed, ask us — we onboard new systems regularly.
How do you handle patient privacy and HIPAA?+
All agents sign NDAs and operate under strict data handling protocols. We execute a BAA (Business Associate Agreement) with every dental clinic before we begin. All work is performed through secured, access-controlled platforms — no patient data on personal devices.
Will your agents actually reduce no-shows?+
Yes — but we'll give you honest numbers. Our multi-touch reminder sequences (call + text + email) have reduced no-show rates by an average of 38% for our dental clients within 60 days. Results vary by clinic size, market, and how consistently the sequences are run.
How fast can you go live?+
Most clinics are fully live within 7 business days of signing. This includes system access setup, agent training on your workflows and scripts, and a supervised launch period before going fully independent.
Can you cover our clinic's specific hours?+
Yes. We have agents across multiple time zones and can provide coverage during your specific clinic hours — including early mornings, evenings, Saturdays, and after-hours call capture.
Do you work with single-location practices or only large groups?+
Both. We work with single-location practices that want to stop losing patients to voicemail, all the way up to multi-location groups that want centralized, consistent patient communication across every office.
Are there long-term contracts?+
No. All engagements are month-to-month with 30 days notice to cancel. We earn your business every month based on results — not a contract lock-in.
