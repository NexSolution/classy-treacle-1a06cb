FAQ

Common Questions.

What scheduling systems do you work with?+
We work with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve Dental, Carestream, and most major practice management systems. If yours isn't listed, ask — we onboard new systems regularly.
How do you handle patient privacy and HIPAA?+
All agents sign NDAs and operate under strict data handling protocols. We execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every clinic before we begin. All work is done through secured, access-controlled platforms — no patient data on personal devices.
Will your agents actually reduce no-shows?+
Our multi-touch reminder sequences have reduced no-show rates by an average of 38% for our dental clients within 60 days. Results vary by clinic size and market, but consistent reminders make a measurable difference every time.
How fast can you go live?+
Most clinics are fully live within 7 business days — that includes system access, agent training on your workflows and scripts, and a supervised launch period before going fully independent.
Can you cover our specific clinic hours?+
Yes. We have agents across multiple time zones and can provide coverage during your exact clinic hours, including early mornings, evenings, Saturdays, and after-hours call capture.
Do you work with single-location practices or only large groups?+
Both. We work with single-location practices that want to stop losing patients to voicemail, all the way up to multi-location groups that want centralized, consistent patient communication across every office.
Are there long-term contracts?+
No. All engagements are month-to-month with 30 days notice to cancel. We earn your business every month based on results — not a contract lock-in.
How do we get started?+
Click "Book Free Strategy Call" anywhere on this page, or email us at supportnexsolution@gmail.com. We'll schedule a 15-minute call, learn about your practice, and send a custom proposal within 48 hours.
