Security & Data Protection

Your Data Is Safe With Us

We understand the responsibility of handling your customer data. Our security practices are built into every aspect of our operation.

🔐 Access Controls

Role-based access and minimum-privilege permissions for all agents. We work within whatever access framework you define.

📄 Confidentiality

All agents sign individual NDAs. Mutual NDAs executed with every client before onboarding begins.

🛡️ Data Handling

No customer data stored on personal devices. All work performed through secured, access-controlled platforms.

🔍 Audit Trail

All customer interactions logged with timestamps for compliance review and quality assurance.

⚠️ Incident Response

Documented breach notification procedures. Any suspected data incident escalated and reported within 24 hours.

✅ Compliance

HIPAA-aware workflows, PHIPA/PIPEDA compliance capabilities, and BAA execution available for healthcare clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does NexSolution provide?+
We provide customer support (email, chat, phone, social media), lead generation and appointment booking, ad campaign management, content creation, and back-office solutions including data entry and order processing.
Can you provide dedicated agents?+
Yes. Every client gets dedicated agents who are trained exclusively on their business, products, and brand voice. Your agents don't split time with other clients.
How fast can you go live?+
Most clients are fully live within 7 business days of signing. This includes discovery, system setup, agent training, and supervised launch.
Can your team work our business hours?+
Yes. We have agents across multiple time zones and can provide coverage during your specific business hours, including evenings and weekends if needed.
How do you handle customer data?+
All agents sign NDAs, work through secured platforms, and follow strict data handling protocols. We offer BAA execution for healthcare clients and comply with HIPAA, PHIPA, and PIPEDA requirements.
Can you scale the team as we grow?+
Absolutely. We have 15+ trained agents ready to deploy. You can start with 2 and scale to 20 as your volume increases. Scaling up typically takes 3-5 business days.
Are there long-term contracts?+
No. All our engagements are month-to-month. We earn your business every month. You can cancel with 30 days notice.
How do we get started?+
Fill out the form above or email support@nexsolution.org. We'll schedule a discovery call, learn about your business, and put together a tailored proposal within 48 hours.
