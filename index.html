How We Work

Your Team Goes Live in 7 Days.
Not 7 Weeks.

We've onboarded dozens of brands. Our process is fast, proven, and built for zero downtime.

01

🔍 Discovery

We learn your brand, products, policies, tone of voice, and backend systems. Your knowledge base starts on day one.

02

⚙️ Configure

We set up your helpdesk, SLAs, macros, escalation flows, and connect to your Shopify or order management system.

03

🎧 Train & Shadow

Your dedicated team completes brand immersion and handles live tickets under supervision. Every single response reviewed.

04

🚀 Launch

Full independent operation with QA checks, weekly reporting, and continuous optimization. We get better every week.

Every agent studies your brand guidelines, past conversations, and tone preferences. We run calibration sessions until responses are indistinguishable from your internal team.

We build a comprehensive knowledge base covering every scenario — from common sizing questions to complex return edge cases — so your team always has the right answer.

🔒 Security & Access

NDAs signed before any access. Agent permissions scoped to minimum required. All data handling follows strict protocols. We work within whatever access controls you define.

📊 Weekly QA & Reporting

Internal quality audits every week. Agents scored on accuracy, tone, speed, and compliance. Full transparency reports delivered every Monday morning.

Contractors & Trades — Deep Dive
