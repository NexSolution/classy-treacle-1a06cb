The Process

7 Things We Do For You

1

Instagram Presence

Your clinic needs an active Instagram page. If you already have one, great — we take it from there. If not, we'll guide you through setting it up. A strong Instagram presence is the foundation of your local visibility.

Requirement: Active account
2

Content Creation

You record short video clips of your practice — your team, your environment, a procedure walkthrough. We handle everything after that: editing, captions, graphics, and posting on a consistent schedule that builds your audience over time.

You record — we handle the rest
3

Online Booking System

Patients click your booking link, choose their service, pick a date and time, and add any notes. You receive an instant notification on your phone, approve the appointment, and the patient is confirmed. No phone tag, no back and forth — just a filled schedule. If you already have a booking system in place, we work with what you have. If not, we set up ours for you.

One-time setup · Lifetime access · Upgrades available
4

Patient Reactivation Campaign

Lapsed patients are one of the most cost-effective growth levers a practice has. We'll need a list of every patient seen at your clinic in the past 6 years. Our specialist will run a dedicated outreach campaign — calls, follow-ups, and reminders — to bring those patients back into your chair.

Requirement: 6-year patient list
5

Your Dedicated Specialist

Every clinic we work with gets one NexSolution specialist — someone who learns your practice, handles all patient-facing communication, and confirms appointments directly through your booking system. We also identify the highest-potential service in your clinic: a premium procedure that's underperforming or one that could dramatically increase revenue with the right push. We build a focused campaign around it. Consultations start at $50.

1 specialist per clinic · Starting at $50/consultation
6

Meta Advertising Campaigns

We plan, build, and manage your Facebook and Instagram ads — from audience targeting and creative to testing and optimization. You allocate a monthly ad budget toward your campaigns. We handle everything on the execution side so your money is working as efficiently as possible.

We run it · You fund the ad budget
7

Shared Tracking & Reporting

Every appointment confirmed, every reactivation call made, every campaign result — logged in a live shared mastersheet. Both your team and ours have full access. You'll always know exactly what's happening and what's working, without having to ask.

Full visibility · Shared access
